## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Software Developer IV** at the **Oromia Innovation and Technology Bureau** with a BSc in Computer Science from Mattu University (CGPA 3.47/4.00). I design, build, and deploy secure web and mobile systems for universities, research institutes, and government bodies, and I like turning manual, paper-based processes into clean, reliable software.

- 🔭 **Building now:** library, archive, and property management platforms for public institutions
- 🧱 **Focus:** clean architecture, secure APIs, and well-designed relational data
- 🌍 **Based in:** Addis Ababa, Ethiopia
- 💬 **Ask me about:** ASP.NET Core, Django, PostgreSQL, Next.js, React Native, system design
